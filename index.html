01 / RESEARCH

From landscape change to evidence people can use.

I am interested in how climate change reshapes mountain environments through flooding, landslides, glacier retreat, and sediment movement—and how research can be made useful to the communities living with those changes.

01

Watershed hazards

Tracing how extreme weather, glacier retreat, and sediment mobilization reshape mountain landscapes and hazard exposure.

02

Geospatial observation

Using GIS, UAV LiDAR, field monitoring, and mapping to connect environmental change with place-based evidence.

03

Community adaptation

Communicating hazard research clearly and collaboratively with communities, partners, and the wider public.
