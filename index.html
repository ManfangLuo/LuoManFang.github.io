01 RESEARCH

I am interested in the environmental changes people can see—and the data, maps, and relationships needed to understand what they mean.

My work asks how glacier retreat, flooding, landslides, and sediment movement transform mountain watersheds. I am especially drawn to research that is scientifically rigorous while remaining accountable to the people and places it concerns.

01

Climate hazards
