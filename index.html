I study climate-driven landscape change in mountain watersheds, using spatial methods and field-based observations to understand risk, water, and place.

My interests include glacier retreat, flooding, landslides, and sediment movement—and how these processes matter to the people living with environmental change. I work with GIS, remote sensing, UAV LiDAR, and research communication to connect technical evidence with accessible public understanding.

  • Climate hazards and changing mountain landscapes
  • GIS, remote sensing, UAV LiDAR, and field monitoring
  • Place-based research communication and StoryMaps
